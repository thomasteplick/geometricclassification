Geometric Tomography Parameters

For a New Geometric Object select the geometric object. For Zoom select the Zoom Axis and enter the Zoom Plane (0-49). For overview of the plane tomography enter the Plane Step (1-4) and starting planes for axes i, j, and k. To rotate the geometric object about an axis, check Rotate Planes, enter the Rotation Axis, and enter Rotation Angle in degrees (+/- 180). To reload the geometric object, check Reset Geometric.
